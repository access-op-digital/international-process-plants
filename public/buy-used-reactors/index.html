Material of construction decides whether a reactor survives your process stream, and it is the first filter a process engineer applies. IPP indexes its reactor inventory across 69 filterable materials of construction, and the four families below cover the bulk of stocked units.

Buying Glass-Lined Reactors

Glass-lined steel puts an inert glass barrier between the process and the shell, so the vessel handles the acids and halides that attack bare stainless. IPP stocks glass-lined reactors from 300 to 10,000 gallons, and every unit in that range is ASME rated. Pfaudler and De Dietrich units both appear in current stock, including a reglassed 5,000 gallon Pfaudler KC body and a 3,050 gallon De Dietrich CE10000.

IPP stocks 316L reactors from 100 to 20,000 gallons, and stocked units reach 145 psi internal at 232.2 °C. Interior finishes run to 240 grit buff polish on the unused Gale Process Solutions vessels.
